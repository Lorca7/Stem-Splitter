STEM SPLITTER - INSTALLATION (macOS 11 or later, tested on macOS 14 Sonoma)
==========================================================================

1. Standalone app
   Drag "Stem Splitter.app" onto the Applications folder.

2. Plug-ins (optional, for use inside a DAW)
   VST3:       copy "Plug-ins/Stem Splitter.vst3"      to  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
   Audio Unit: copy "Plug-ins/Stem Splitter.component" to  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components
   (In Finder press Cmd+Shift+G and paste the folder path.)

3. First launch
   This build is not notarised by Apple, so macOS will say it "cannot be opened". Either:
     - Right-click (or Control-click) Stem Splitter.app, choose Open, then click Open, or
     - open Terminal and run:
         xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Stem Splitter.app"
   Do the same for the plug-ins if your DAW refuses to load them:
         xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/"Stem Splitter.vst3"
         xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/"Stem Splitter.component"

4. Models
   The 4-stem and 6-stem neural networks are included inside the app. The plug-ins use the copies
   inside /Applications/Stem Splitter.app, so install the app even if you only use the plug-ins.
   The "4 Stems HQ" models (about 324 MB) download from inside the app the first time you pick them.

QUICK START
-----------
  1. Drop a WAV file onto the window (or click to browse).
  2. Pick an engine at the top (4 Stems, 6 Stems, 4 Stems HQ, Lite) and press "Separate".
  3. Listen: mute (M) / solo (S) stems, set their levels, click a waveform to jump.
  4. Export, in the panel on the right:
       Stems       - one WAV per selected stem.
       Custom mix  - one WAV of what you hear: click instruments to remove them (e.g. "Instrumental"),
                     and/or switch on "Remove frequencies" to cut a low, high or band of frequencies.
  You can also drag a stem's handle (the dots on the right of each lane) straight into Finder or a DAW.

Keyboard: Space play/pause, Return back to start, L loop, Left/Right skip 5 s,
          Cmd+O open, Cmd+E export, Esc cancel.
